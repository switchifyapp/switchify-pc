title: Home menu
description: Press Select to open Home, the one scanning menu that leads to everything Switchify PC can do, including windows, editing, browser, media, zoom, display and scanning actions.
---

Press and release **Select** to open Home. Home is the one menu that leads to every action: the other panels have a **Home** key that brings you back here. It scans through these items; press Select on the one you want. Home opens whether or not another app is in front, and it never takes focus away from your work. It opens on the display the pointer is on, and moves with the pointer if something moves it to another display while Home is open.

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Point** | Start [point scanning](/using/point-scanning/) to choose a spot on screen. |
| **Mouse** | Open the [mouse panel](/using/mouse-panel/) to move the pointer step by step. |
| **Keyboard** | Open the scanning [keyboard](/using/keyboard/). |
| **Apps and windows** | Switch app forward or backward, open the app overview, show the desktop, minimise, maximise or restore (toggle full screen on a Mac), close the window, and shut down or restart the computer. |
| **Editing** | Copy, cut, paste, select all, undo, redo, save, find. |
| **Browser** | Back, forward, reload, new tab, close tab, reopen a closed tab, next and previous tab, address bar. |
| **Media** | Play or pause, next and previous track, volume up and down, mute. |
| **Zoom** | Zoom in, zoom out and reset zoom in the app in front, using the Ctrl or Cmd shortcuts with =, - and 0. Browsers, PDF readers, mail apps and many other apps zoom with these keys. Some apps use them for something else; Word, for example, does not zoom. |
| **Displays** | **Next display** or **Previous display** starts point scanning on another display. On the mouse panel, the Monitor keys under Speed and more move the pointer instead. |
| **Scanning** | **Open Switchify** to show the Switchify PC window, then pause, reverse direction, change the scan speed and line speed, and switch between line-only and grid-then-line point scanning. Home stays open; the next point scan uses the method you chose. |
| **My panels** | Shown once you have made panels of your own. Opens a list of them; choose one to open it. A panel stays open after each action, so you can choose the next one, until you choose a tile that goes Back or closes it. |
| **Close menu** | Close Home and stop scanning. **Close menu** does the same on every menu. |

Home and the menus it opens can be rearranged in the [Panels](/using/panels/) section of the Switchify PC window, and put back the way they came. The table above describes them before any change.

## Shut down or restart

**Shut down** and **Restart** are the last items in Apps and windows. Each opens a page of its own with three tiles: **Back**, then **Shut down now** or **Restart now**, then **Close menu**. Scanning on that page starts on Back, or on Close menu if your scan direction is reversed, so a Select that lands early never shuts down or restarts. Nothing happens until you choose the middle tile.

Switchify PC asks Windows or macOS to shut down or restart and lets open apps close normally. Apps may ask you to save first, and a message from the computer may hold the shutdown until you deal with it. Nothing is forced closed. On a Mac, the first time you use Shut down or Restart, macOS asks whether Switchify PC may control System Events; choose Allow. If you chose Don't Allow, nothing happens when you choose Shut down now or Restart now, and the menu may show that the action failed. Turn it on again in System Settings under Privacy & Security, Automation, Switchify PC.

In Settings you can choose whether Select opens Home every time, or returns to the last mode you used.

Home and the menus it opens have a slim bar along their top, with the menu's name on the left and a **Settings** button on the right. Click the button with a mouse to open the Switchify PC window on its Settings page. The menu stays where it is while the pointer is over the bar, even when it is set to move out of the pointer's way. The bar itself never takes focus; the Switchify PC window opens in front of your work.

## Colours

Each built-in panel has a colour, and the bar along its top shows it beside the panel's name. Home's tiles share the colour of the panel they open, so the colour you choose on Home is the colour you see when the panel opens.

- Blue: the keyboard.
- Green: the mouse panel, More clicks and the pages after you choose a point. Home's **Point** tile is green too.
- Purple: Apps and windows, with Shut down and Restart.
- Orange: Editing.
- Pink: Browser and Tabs.
- Red: Media.
- Yellow: Zoom and Displays.
- Grey: Scanning.

Home, My panels and your own panels have plain bars. To turn the colours off, turn off **Colour the built-in panels** in [Scanning settings](/using/scanning-settings/). If you [rearrange Home](/using/panels/#rearrange-the-built-in-menus), its tiles keep the colours they had when you saved, and you can change or clear them in the editor.

Nothing else is written across a menu: the highlight shows what Select will choose. While a menu is waiting or paused, nothing is highlighted; press Select to carry on. Only a failure is written out: if an action could not be done, a short message appears beside the menu and goes away when you deal with it.
