---
title: Mouse panel and radar
description: Move the pointer step by step from a scanning panel, repeat movement by holding, and send the pointer across the screen with Radar.
---

Choose **Mouse** from [Home](/using/home-menu/), or give a switch **Mouse** under **Open a panel**. A panel of scanning buttons appears around a ring that marks the pointer, on the display the pointer is on. It follows the pointer from display to display.

## What the panel offers

- **Move** in eight directions.
- **Left click**, **Right click**, **Double click**.
- **Start drag** and **End drag**.
- **Scroll up** and **Scroll down**.
- **Speed and more**, a second page with **Slower** and **Faster** pointer speed, **Blocks** to switch between gliding and moving in blocks, **More clicks** (middle, triple, Shift, Ctrl, Alt and Windows or Command click at the pointer), **Monitor left, right, up, down** to jump the pointer to another display, and a choice of where the panel sits. Speed shows the current level, from 1 to 10, and a press moves it half a level. **Movement** returns to the first page. After a click from More clicks, or **Back**, the panel comes back on its first page.
- **Home** to open [Home](/using/home-menu/), for editing, windows, browser, media, zoom and the rest, and **My panels** once you have made [panels](/using/panels/) of your own. Choose **Mouse** on Home to bring the panel back.
- **Keyboard** to open the scanning [keyboard](/using/keyboard/).
- **Close** to stop scanning. To change to [point scanning](/using/point-scanning/) instead, choose **Home**, then **Point**.
- Rows and columns of your own tiles, if you added any in the [Panels](/using/panels/) section. Rows sit just above the bottom row; columns run down the right of the keys.

The panel has a slim bar along its top with the Switchify PC name and a **Settings** button, then the buttons. Nothing else is written on it: the highlight shows what Select will choose. While the panel is waiting or paused, or the pointer is moving until you stop it, nothing is highlighted; press Select to carry on. Only a failure is written out: if an action could not be done, a short message appears just above the panel, or below it when the panel is at the top of the display, and goes away when you deal with it.

## Speed and repeat

Pointer speed has ten levels plus fine tuning. The panel, the **Mouse pointer** section at the end of the Scanning page, and the Android app all show the same level. Hold a switch on a movement or scroll button and it repeats, with a repeat interval and gradual acceleration you can set. Repeat stops when you release the switch, or on the next press, whichever you choose under Stop repeating in the Mouse pointer section.

## Move in blocks

With **Move in blocks** on, the arrows jump the pointer a set distance instead of gliding. Turn it on or off with the **Blocks** tile on the Speed and more page, with **Move in blocks** in the **Mouse pointer** section, or with a switch set to **Move in blocks on / off** (under **Mouse** in the switch action list). The tile shows On or Off.

The switch action works while scanning is running, whether or not the mouse panel is open. It saves the change, and scanning keeps its place.

- With repeat off, each press moves the pointer one block.
- With repeat on, the pointer hops one block at a time, at the mouse panel's scan speed, until you stop it the same way you stop a glide.

Choose **Small**, **Medium** or **Large** blocks in the Mouse pointer section. A block is a twentieth, a tenth or a fifth of the shorter side of the display the pointer is on. Pointer speed doesn't change the size of a block.

The panel can move itself out of the way when the pointer approaches it.

## Clicking

**Left click**, **Right click** and **Double click** click wherever the pointer is. The panel stays on screen while it clicks. Clicks pass through the panel, so a click with the pointer over the panel reaches the window behind it. The [Settings bar](#settings-bar) is the exception: with the pointer over the bar, the panel is hidden for a moment while it clicks, so the click reaches the window behind the bar rather than opening Settings.

To left click without scanning to the Left click tile, give a switch the **Left click at the pointer** action, under **Mouse** in the switch action list. It clicks wherever the pointer is, with the panel open or not. It's handy with [Move in blocks](#move-in-blocks): hop the pointer onto the target, then press the click switch.

With this panel, the keyboard or Home open, or while scanning waits for Select, scanning stays where it was after the click. Other menus, custom panels and point scans act on the window they began over. With one of those under way, a click that brings another window to the front resets scanning; start again with Select.

The click switch doesn't click:

- while a drag is held. The drag carries on. On this panel, the message above the panel says the action failed; press Select to carry on.
- while the pointer is moving or scrolling until you stop it. The press stops it, as any switch does.
- during an Auto select countdown. It opens the action menu, as other switches do.

## Settings bar

Every scanning panel has a slim bar along its top, with the Switchify PC name on the left and a **Settings** button on the right. Click the button with a mouse to open the Switchify PC window on its Settings page. The panel stays where it is while the pointer is over the bar, even when it is set to move out of the pointer's way. The bar itself never takes focus; the Switchify PC window opens in front of your work, as it does from the tray or menu bar.

## Radar

Radar sits in the middle of the panel's arrows and reaches any point in three presses:

1. Dashed lines divide the display into quarters. They highlight in turn with a rounded outline, then the whole display. Press Select to choose one; the pointer moves to its centre.
2. A coloured line sweeps clockwise from a ring at that centre. It has a dark edge so it shows on light and dark windows, and it gets thicker or thinner with the mouse panel's **Highlight thickness**. Press Select to fix the direction.
3. The line grows out along that direction towards the edge of the area, and the pointer travels with its tip so you can see what it is over. A faint dotted line shows how much further it can go. Press Select to leave the pointer there.

The panel then comes back. A drag in progress stays held, so Radar can drop something a long way away.
