---
title: Mouse panel and radar
description: Move the pointer step by step from a scanning panel, repeat movement by holding, and send the pointer across the screen with Radar.
---

Choose **Mouse** from [Home](/using/home-menu/), or give a switch the **Open mouse** action. A panel of scanning buttons appears around a ring that marks the pointer, on the display the pointer is on. It follows the pointer from display to display.

## What the panel offers

- **Move** in eight directions.
- **Left click**, **Right click**, **Double click**.
- **Start drag** and **End drag**.
- **Scroll up** and **Scroll down**.
- **Slower** and **Faster** pointer speed. Each shows the current level, from 1 to 10, and a press moves it half a level.
- **Monitor left, right, up, down** to jump the pointer to another display.
- **Actions** for the same editing, window, browser and media items as Home, and Home itself. Zoom is in Home: choose **Home**, then **Zoom**, and choose **Mouse** afterwards to bring the panel back.
- **Keyboard** to open the scanning [keyboard](/using/keyboard/).
- **Switch to Point** to change to [point scanning](/using/point-scanning/).

## Speed and repeat

Pointer speed has ten levels plus fine tuning. The panel, the Mouse settings page and the Android app all show the same level. Hold a switch on a movement or scroll button and it repeats, with a repeat interval and gradual acceleration you can set. Repeat stops when you release the switch, or on the next press, whichever you choose in Settings.

The panel can move itself out of the way when the pointer approaches it.

## Settings bar

Every scanning panel has a slim bar along its top, with the Switchify PC name on the left and a **Settings** button on the right. Click the button with a mouse to open the Switchify PC window on its Settings page. The panel stays where it is while the pointer is over the bar, even when it is set to move out of the pointer's way. The bar itself never takes focus; the Switchify PC window opens in front of your work, as it does from the tray or menu bar.

## Radar

Radar sits in the middle of the panel's arrows and reaches any point in three presses:

1. The display is divided into quarters. They highlight in turn, then the whole display. Press Select to choose one; the pointer moves to its centre.
2. A dotted beam sweeps clockwise round that centre. Press Select to fix the direction.
3. The beam grows out along that direction towards the edge of the area, and the pointer travels with its tip so you can see what it is over. Press Select to leave the pointer there.

The panel then comes back. A drag in progress stays held, so Radar can drop something a long way away.
