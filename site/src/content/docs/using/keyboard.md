description: Switchify PC's own scanning keyboard, with three layouts, modifier keys, automatic spacing and capitals, and word prediction.

Switchify PC has its own on-screen keyboard that scans with your switches. It is not the operating system's on-screen keyboard, it never takes focus, and it does not accept mouse clicks.

Open it from [Home](/using/home-menu/), from the point scan action menu, from the mouse panel, or with a switch set to **Open keyboard**. The text goes wherever the cursor already is.

## Typing

Rows highlight in turn. Press Select on a row, then on a key. The escape slot at the end of a row returns to the rows. After a key, scanning starts again from the first row, or from the key you just typed if you set **Start again from** to *where I selected* in Settings.

The keyboard stays open after Space, Backspace, Enter and Tab, so you can keep writing. **Close** takes you back to wherever you opened it from: the mouse panel, Home, or the scan you were in.

## Layouts

Choose in Settings or from the keyboard's footer:

- **QWERTY**, the full layout.
- **Simple QWERTY**, fewer keys per row.
- **Common letters first**, the most used letters nearest the start of each scan.

## Pages

- **Letters**, with a few punctuation keys: comma, full stop, apostrophe and question mark (Simple QWERTY has just the full stop).
- **Navigation**: Escape, F1 to F12, arrows, Home, End, Page Up, Page Down, Delete and more.
- **Numbers**: 0 to 9, a decimal point, and `+` `-` `*` `/`.
- **Symbols**: every symbol from a standard UK keyboard on its own key, so nothing is hidden behind Shift. Each key types what it shows. Common punctuation such as `@` `.` `,` `?` `!` comes first, then brackets, then maths signs, then `£` `$` `&` `#` and the rarer symbols.

## Modifiers

Shift, Ctrl, Alt (Option on a Mac) and Windows (Command on a Mac) each cycle through *off*, *once* and *locked*. **Caps** locks capitals.

## Automatic spacing and capitals

After a full stop, comma, question mark or exclamation mark on the Letters or Symbols page, Switchify PC adds a space for you and removes a space it typed just before the mark. It also capitalises the next letter after a full stop, question mark or exclamation mark. Other symbols, including the colon and semicolon, type exactly as shown. If you need a full stop with no space after it, as in a web or email address, use Backspace after it, or the full stop on the Numbers page.

## Repeating keys

Backspace, Delete, the arrows, Tab, Page Up and Page Down can repeat while you hold the switch.

## Where it sits

Dock the keyboard to any of nine positions on the display. It keeps working when you switch apps, and it refits itself if the taskbar or Dock changes.

Word prediction has its own page: [Word prediction](/using/word-prediction/).
