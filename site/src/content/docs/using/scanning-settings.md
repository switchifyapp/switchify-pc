These settings are on the **Scanning** page. They save as you change them and apply straight away. The row of tiles at the top of the page shows what each section is set to; choose one to go to it, or type what you are looking for in **Find a setting**.

Chosen options in Settings have a filled background and a checkmark. A separate outline shows which control has keyboard focus. The scanner colour still controls the highlight on the scanning panels.

## Movement

- **Automatic** scanning moves by itself. The rate can be anything from 0.1 to 10 seconds per step, in 0.1 second steps. It needs one switch set to Select.
- **Manual** scanning moves only when you press Next or Previous. It needs Select, Next and Previous.
- **Initial direction** can be forward or reverse.
- **Pass limit**: stop after 1, 2, 3, 5 passes, or never. The default is 3.
- **Extra time on the first item**: off, or 0.5 to 2 seconds. The first item stays highlighted for longer when scanning starts, starts again, or moves into a row or group, giving you time to react.
- **Inside a row or group**: the same rate, 1.5 times as long, or twice as long. Items inside a row or group stay highlighted for this long compared with the scan rate. Rows and groups themselves move at the scan rate.

The last two are shared by menus, the keyboard and the mouse panel, and only change automatic scanning. Point scanning keeps its own line speed.

## Item scanning

How menus and the keyboard are grouped:

- **Groups, then items**: choose a group, then an item in it.
- **Rows, groups, then items**: long rows of seven or more items are split into two to four groups first.
- **One item at a time**.

## After a selection

- **After a selection**: keep scanning, or wait for Select.
- **Start again from**: the beginning, or where I selected.

When the keyboard or the mouse panel opens, it follows its own **After a selection** choice. Set to wait, the panel opens without moving and starts scanning from the beginning when you press Select. This also applies when you go back to the mouse panel, for example after closing the keyboard.

Choosing **Back** in a menu opens the menu above it afresh, scanning from the beginning like any menu you open. It does not carry on from the item you chose before.

## Appearance

- **Scanner colour**: red, green, blue or yellow. Saved white scan highlights change to blue.
- **Highlight thickness**: thin, standard or thick.
- **Panel size**: small, medium or large.
- **Panel theme**: match system, light or dark. This colours the keyboard, the mouse panel, Home, the action menus and [your own panels](/using/panels/). **Match system** follows your computer's light or dark setting, and the panels change when it does. On light panels the scanner colour is drawn in a deeper shade so the highlight stays easy to see.
- Panels can move away from the pointer.

## Different settings for menus and the keyboard

Menus and the keyboard can each have their own movement, rate and pattern, or use the shared defaults. A reset returns them to the defaults.
