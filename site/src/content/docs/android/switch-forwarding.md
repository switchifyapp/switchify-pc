---
title: Switch Forwarding
description: Send presses and releases from switches connected to your Android device straight to the computer, mapped by a profile.
---

Switch Forwarding sends the press and release of up to eight switches connected to your Android device to the computer, where a profile turns each one into a key, a shortcut, a mouse button, a scroll or a media action. Other software on the computer, such as Grid 3, sees them as if the switch were plugged into the computer.

## Start

1. [Pair](/android/pairing/) the device.
2. In Switchify on Android, assign the **Open PC Switch Forwarding** action to a switch, or open it from PC control.
3. Pick a profile and start. Stop at any time, change profiles, or go back to normal Switchify scanning on the phone.

## Profiles

Switchify PC comes with two:

- **Generic keyboard** maps the switches to keys such as Space, so most switch-aware software works at once.
- **Grid 3** talks to Smartbox Grid 3 directly. Windows only.

Built-in profiles are read-only. Duplicate one to make your own, then map each of the eight switches to:

- a key;
- a shortcut of up to four keys;
- a mouse button, held or clicked;
- a scroll action;
- a media action.

Open the profile manager from the main window, the tray or menu bar, or Settings. Profiles sync to your other computers when you sign in.
