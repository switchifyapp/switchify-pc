---
title: PC control
description: Control the computer's pointer, keyboard and windows from Switchify on Android.
---

PC control is built into Switchify on Android. Once [paired](/android/pairing/), its Mouse, Typing and Window surfaces drive the computer, and Switchify PC carries out each action.

## Mouse

Move the pointer in eight directions, click, double click, right click, start and end a drag, and scroll. Set the pointer speed, hold to repeat movement, and step between monitors.

## Typing

Type live into the focused control, or write a draft and send it when you are ready. Send common keys, shortcuts, held modifiers, and repeating navigation keys such as the arrows, Tab and Backspace.

## Window

Next and previous app, task view, show desktop, minimise, maximise and close, with Ctrl, Alt, Shift and Start modifiers for shortcuts.

## Settings on the computer

These apply while a phone is controlling the computer:

- **Pointer speed**: a slider from Slow to Fast, shown as a level from 1 to 10. **Fine tune speed** moves it in smaller steps with **Slower** and **Faster**.
- **Repeat**: movement, scrolling and key repeat, with an interval and gradual acceleration.
- **Click when I stop**: a left click after the pointer stops moving, with a countdown you can set.
- **Cursor overlay**: show a high-visibility marker or crosshairs, choose its size and one of five colours, and show it on input or whenever the phone is controlling.

## Remote scanning

The phone's switches can also drive the computer's own scanning. On the phone's Forwarding screen choose **Switchify scanning** instead of a profile, start, then press Select: Switchify PC's point scanning, menus and keyboard run exactly as they do for local switches, including hold actions. Up to eight phone switches take part. The computer's own switch keys are inactive during the session, and Escape remains the emergency stop.

While remote scanning runs, the Mouse, Typing and Window surfaces on the phone are unavailable. Stop Switchify scanning on the phone to use them again.

The session ends when the phone disconnects, when you choose **Disconnect** on the computer, or when the computer hears nothing from the phone's switches for 5 seconds.

### Set up the phone's switches on the computer

Switchify PC decides what each phone switch does. On the Switches page, under **Mobile switches**, there are eight numbered slots. Slot 1 is switch 1 on the phone's Forwarding screen, slot 2 is switch 2, and so on; they are positions, not detected switches. Each slot can have a name (otherwise it is **Remote switch 1**, **Remote switch 2** and so on), a press action, and a list of hold actions, just like a switch connected to the computer. Choose **Add remote switch** to set up an empty slot.

Out of the box, slots 1 to 6 are **Select**, **Next**, **Previous**, **Pause / resume**, **Reverse direction** and **Stop scanning**, and slots 7 and 8 are empty. You can change or remove any of them. Switchify PC may label these switches **Remote** or **Switchify Remote**; both mean the Switchify app on your phone.
